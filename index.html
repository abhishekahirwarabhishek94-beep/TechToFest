> ARCHITECTURE_01

Shared Timestamp Consolidation

On 22/11/26 at 11:00 AM, both Rock with your Robo and Cyber Circuit Debugging take place. Rather than duplicating 11:00 AM on separate lines, a single time anchor anchors both parallel sessions.

> ARCHITECTURE_02

Distinct Color & Venue Badging

Rock with your Robo carries a blue Robotics badge at IT Garden, whereas Cyber Circuit Debugging features a green Tech badge at Electronics Lab 3. Color & venue differences eliminate physical confusion instantly.

> ARCHITECTURE_03

Side-by-Side Parallel Grid

Concurrent sessions expand into responsive side-by-side cards with an attention-grabbing ⚡ PARALLEL_SLOT // CHOOSE_TRACK flag, clearly signaling parallel attendance choices to participants.

> ARCHITECTURE_04

Instant Track Isolation

Attendees exclusively interested in Robotics or Technical research can toggle the Category or Day dropdowns at any time to instantly hide the parallel track, zeroing out visual clutter.
